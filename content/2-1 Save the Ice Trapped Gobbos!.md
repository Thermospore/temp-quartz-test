# Cutscene breaks
All three cutscenes in this level can be [[Cutscene Break|CSB]]'d with [[Magic Eye Zoomers|Binos]]. Two of them were found by [[Turbomost|Turbomošt]] and are described below < the third has been known for a while — find the original source and write it up >

## Tree cutting
Carry your momentum into the trigger zone while using Binos. Let Croc's residual momentum carry you in, the same as the [[1-B2 Cannon Boat Keith|Keith]] CSBs and the [[Inca Hub]] burp minigame

[[Ashen Deimos]] [PS1 Any% guide](https://youtu.be/d6igQ43ZZAc?si=WUKuBInC_ww_C2Lx&t=111) has a good visual of where the trigger is

Originally done with a *sidestep* into the trigger ([2026-05-27](https://discord.com/channels/313375426112389123/408694062862958592/1509232340377800996)), but that turned out to be unnecessary

## Ice block carving
Consistent setup ([2026-07-20](https://discord.com/channels/313375426112389123/408694062862958592/1528844636544565310)):

1. jump high, so you are pushing forward against the invisible wall at the top of the door
2. triple jump without moving forward
3. you are now just short of the cutscene trigger — tap forward and hold Binos

If you just run through the door, Croc keeps some momentum on the other side and walks himself into the cutscene
