# Cutscene breaks
All three cutscenes in this level can be [[Cutscene Break|CSB]]'d with [[Magic Eye Zoomers|Binos]]. ## Tree cutting
Carry your momentum into the trigger zone while using Binos. Let Croc's residual momentum carry you in, the same as the [[1-B2 Cannon Boat Keith|Keith]] CSBs and the [[Inca Hub]] burp minigame

[[Ashen Deimos]] [PS1 Any% guide](https://youtu.be/d6igQ43ZZAc?si=WUKuBInC_ww_C2Lx&t=111) has a good visual of where the trigger is

Originally done with a *sidestep* into the trigger ([2026-05-27](https://discord.com/channels/313375426112389123/408694062862958592/1509232340377800996)), but that turned out to be unnecessary

## Ice block carving
If you can break out of the ice block carving cutscene, you are free to clip into the iceblock and grab the bomb barrel early!

To [[Cutscene Break|CSB]], we need to let Croc's residual momentum carry us into the cutscene trigger area while using the [[Magic Eye Zoomers|Binos]] (same as the tree cutting CSB earlier in the level).

However, normally when you run through [[Pasted image 20261103161539.png|this door]], Croc exits with an auto-walk which 1) blocks use of binos and 2) causes him to walk right into the cutscene trigger area.

Luckily, we can cancel the auto-walk by simply jumping out of it (backwards, so we don't enter the cutscene trigger). Then we are free to use the binos to CSB, just like for the tree cutting cutscene.

[Here](https://youtu.be/Gzvv1-_mX4s) is a video demonstration/explanation by [[Thermospore]] ([discord discussion](https://discord.com/channels/313375426112389123/408694062862958592/1573261879051288720))

Some older methods include:
- Triple stomping through the door [[Ice carve skip HD.mp4|like so]] (setup by [[Turbomost|Turbomošt]] [2026-07-20](https://discord.com/channels/313375426112389123/408694062862958592/1528844636544565310))
- Sidestepping through the door (see [this guide](https://youtu.be/d6igQ43ZZAc?si=nupGcbmPQt6d06ig&t=241) by [[Ashen Deimos]] for addl info. Might be [[OG-PSX]] specific)
