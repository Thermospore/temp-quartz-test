# Cutscene breaks
All three cutscenes in this level can be [[Cutscene Break|CSB]]'d with [[Magic Eye Zoomers|Binos]]. ## Tree cutting
Carry your momentum into the trigger zone while using Binos. Let Croc's residual momentum carry you in, the same as the [[1-B2 Cannon Boat Keith|Keith]] CSBs and the [[Inca Hub]] burp minigame

[[Ashen Deimos]] [PS1 Any% guide](https://youtu.be/d6igQ43ZZAc?si=WUKuBInC_ww_C2Lx&t=111) has a good visual of where the trigger is

Originally done with a *sidestep* into the trigger ([2026-05-27](https://discord.com/channels/313375426112389123/408694062862958592/1509232340377800996)), but that turned out to be unnecessary

## Ice block carving
If you can break out of the ice block carving cutscene, you are free to clip into the iceblock and grab the barrel early!

However, normally when you run through [[Pasted image 20261103161539.png|this door]], Croc keeps some momentum on the other side and walks himself into the cutscene trigger area. This prevents you from doing a [[Cutscene Break|CSB]].

A consistent setup was found ([2026-07-20](https://discord.com/channels/313375426112389123/408694062862958592/1528844636544565310)) to prevent Croc from walking himself into the cutscene trigger, allowing for CSB

1. Jump high, so you are pushing forward against the invisible wall at the top of the door
2. Triple jump without moving forward
3. You are now on the other side of the door, just short of the cutscene trigger — tap forward and hold Binos to CSB
4. You can now take the barrel during the cutscene
