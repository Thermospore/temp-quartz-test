When you use the last item in [[Croc's Backpack]], it actually remains in your inventory and you can keep using it infinitely. < when does it stop working? when dying, or leaving the level? >. For example, you can spam [[Jelly Jump|Gummi Jump]]s (which allows for the [[Infinite Jump]] glitch), or keep retrying a [[Clockwork Gobbo]]. Doesn't seem to work for [[Key]]s though.

This glitch is not possible if you collect the [[Magic Eye Zoomers]], because then [[Croc's Backpack]] can never be empty.
