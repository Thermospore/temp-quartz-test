A glitch in [[Caveman Hub]] where you clip into [[3-G Caveman Tribe - Secret Mine]] without meeting the 5 [[Golden Gobbo]] requirement to open the [[Golden Gobbo Door|Jigsaw Door]]

Allows runners to skip 5 trophies in the [[100%]] category. Initial runs were done without a setup and were extremely inconsistent. 

It works in [[Lost Levels|Secret]] [[Caveman Hub]] too, but obviously there is no point (there is no [[Golden Gobbo Door|Jigsaw Door]] to skip through)

Discovered on 2019-09-12 by [[Paulmall]] ([original video](https://discord.com/channels/313375426112389123/408694062862958592/621627275926044672))

In 2020-07, [[Thermospore]] found a consistent [setup](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vZKiRpvqyGs)

In 2024-11, [[Thermospore]] found a faster and more consistent [setup](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cUwTstfa-bk)
