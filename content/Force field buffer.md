# Working theory
- The gobbos have a force-field which pushes you away from them (similar to dantinis; recall the trauma in [[2-2 Chase the Choo Choo Train|Train]]'s [[Golden Gobbo]] level)
- The game tracks the current amount of force applied to you, and does not reset it to 0 upon exiting to [[Main Menu]]
- When you load back into the game, you still have the force applied to you and get a big push
- Presumably, the bug doesn't occur in [[OG-PSX]]/[[HD]] because the force is properly reset upon exiting to main menu (:SnowSad:)
