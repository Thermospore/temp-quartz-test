You can think of these as a currency, which you spend a certain amount of to open a [[Golden Gobbo Door]]. For example, you could use golden gobbos collected in [[Sailor Hub|Sailor]] to get into [[4-G Inca Tribe - Secret Maze|Inca Jigsaw]]

You can grab one while [[Death Abuse|DA]]ing, which respawns you at your last [[Save Gong]] instead of warping you out ([2026-05-28](https://discord.com/channels/313375426112389123/408694062862958592/1509374275130359829), [[Turbomost|Turbomošt]]). There are only two rooms where the Golden Gobbo is close enough to a hazard to do it:
- [[3-4 Climb the Devil's Tower!|Pet]] — saves time, see that page
- [[2-5 KaBooom! It's Roger Red Ant|Roger]] — loses time, because you respawn in the room before the warp point
