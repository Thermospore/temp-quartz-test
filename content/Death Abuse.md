Deliberately dying to send yourself back to your last [[Save Gong]] (or the level entrance if you haven't hit one), which is often faster than walking back. Your progress in the level is kept.

Collectibles grabbed on the way down still count:
- [[Color Crystal|Gem]]s
- [[Golden Gobbo]]s — see that page; only possible in two rooms
- the [[Jigsaw Piece]] in [[1-G Sailor Tribe — Secret Mine|Sailor Jigsaw]], the only one you can reach while dying. You keep it and aren't warped out

Compare: [[Game Over Abuse|GOA]], which kicks you out of the level entirely
