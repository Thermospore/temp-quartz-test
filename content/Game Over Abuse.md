Dying while you have 0 hearts left, to get the Game Over screen and be kicked out of the level. Used in [[Any%]] to leave a level early once its objective is complete, rather than walking back to the exit

It can't be used in [[Max%]], because you have to leave the level normally to bank its 100 [[Crystal]]s

Compare: [[Death Abuse|DA]], which keeps you in the level
